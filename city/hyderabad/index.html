About this reading

Hyderabad, Telangana is one of the 10 cities tracked by the Indian Traffic Premier League. Every hour, we pull live, traffic-aware driving times across Hyderabad's four busiest cross-city corridors — each one forced through the city centre rather than a ring road or bypass, so the number reflects what driving through Hyderabad actually feels like right now, not the fastest possible detour around it.
